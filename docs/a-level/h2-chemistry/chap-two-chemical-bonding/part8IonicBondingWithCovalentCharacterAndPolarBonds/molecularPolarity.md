# **Molecualr Polarity**

## **Bond Polarity**

- In an covalent bond between two atoms of the same element, the electrons are equally shared as the atoms have the **same electronegativity**.

- However, in an covalent bond between two atoms with **different electronegativities**, the electrons are pulled towards to **more electronegative** atom. The electrons are thus not equally shared. This bond is thus **polar**
- This results in **dipole** on the bond and **partial charges** on the atoms

## **Molecular Polarity**

- Depending on the **shape** of the molecule, the individual dipoles may or may not cancel each other completely. (this is similar to addition of vectors in physics)
- If the **net dipole** is 0, the molecule is **non-polar**, else it is **polar**
