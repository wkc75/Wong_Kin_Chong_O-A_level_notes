# **Covalent Bonding and Orbital Overlap**

## **Covalent Bonding**

A covalent bond is formed when two atoms **share a pair of electrons**.

<!--prettier-ignore-->
!!! definition "Definition"
    Covalent bonding is the electrostatic force of attraction between the **negatively charged shared electrons** and the **positively charged nuclei**.

## **Orbital Overlap**

- In order for a covalent bond to be formed, the **orbitals of the two atoms must overlap**.
- The **shared electrons** or **bond pairs** occupy the overlapping region.

### **Sigma, $\sigma$, bond**

- Sigma ($\sigma$) bond is formed by **head-on** overlap of two orbitals

<div id="sigma-bond-overlap-viewer"></div>

### **Pi, $\pi$, bond**

- Pi ($\pi$) bond is formed by **side-on** overlap of two orbitals

<div id="pi-bond-overlap-viewer"></div>

### **Sigma vs Pi bonds**

Pi bond is **weaker** than sigma bond as the degree of overlap is smaller in pi bond.

### **Sigma and Pi bonds in Single, Double and Triple bonds**

- A **single** bond consists of **a $\sigma$ bond**
- A **double** bond consists of **a $\sigma$ bond** and **a $\pi$ bond**
- A **double** bond consists of **a $\sigma$ bond** and **two $\pi$ bond**
