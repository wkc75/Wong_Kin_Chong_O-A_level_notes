# **Ionic Bonding and Structure**

## **Giant Ionic Lattice Structure and Ionic Bonding**

<div id="ionic-lattice-3d-viewer"></div>

Ionic compounds in solid state have a **giant ionic lattice structure** which is lattice of **cations** and **anions** held together by ionic bonding.

**Ionic Bonding** is the **electrostatic force of attraction between oppositely charged ions**.

## **Strength of Ionic Bonding**

### **Lattice Energy**

<!--prettier-ignore-->
!!! definition "Definition"
    **Lattice energy** is defined as the heat evolved when 1 mole of a pure ionic solid is formed from its constituent gaseous ions.

We use lattice energy to compare the strength of ionic bonding.

The larger the magnitude of lattice energy, the stronger the ionic bonding.

The magnitude of lattice energy depends on

1. The **charge** of ions
2. The **radii** of ions

$$
LE \propto \frac{q_+ \times q_-}{r_+ + r_-}
$$

<!--prettier-ignore-->
!!! tip
    **Ionic charge usually affects lattice energy more** than ionic radius because the ionic charges are multiplied together in the numerator.
