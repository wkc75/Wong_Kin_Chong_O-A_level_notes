# **Physicl Properties of Ionic Compounds**

## **High Boiling and Melting Points**

- Ionic Compounds have **giant ionic lattice structure** with **strong ionic bonding** between the oppositely charged ions
- **Large amount of energy** is required to overcome the strong ionic bonding to melt/boil them.
- Thus, ionic compounds have high boiling and melting points.

## **Good Electrical Conductivity in Molten and Aqueous State**

- In **molten / aqueous state**, ionic compounds contain **free moving ions** which can act as **mobile charge carriers**
- Therefore, molten and aqueous ionic compounds can conduct electricity.

<!--prettier-ignore-->
!!! tip
    The electrical conductivity of aqueous ionic compounds increases as concentration of ions increases.

## **Hard but Brittle**

Ionic compounds are **Hard**

- Ionic Compounds have **giant ionic lattice structure** with **strong ionic bonding** between the oppositely charged ions
- **A strong force** is required to overcome the strong ionic bonding.
- Thus, ionic compounds are hard.

<div id="ionic-brittleness-viewer"></div>

Ionic compounds are **Brittle**

- When a large enough force is applied, the layers of ions are displaced.
- **Ions of like charges move closer to one another**.
- The **repulsion between ions of like charges** cause the lattice to shatter.
- Thus, ionic compounds are brittle.

## **Solubility**

Ionic Compounds are soluble in **water** but **not in organic solvent**
