# **Steps to Draw Dot and Cross Diagram for Covalent Molecules**

1. Decide on the **central atom**. The central atom is usually the one with the **lowest electronegativity** or the one that can form **more covalent bonds**

<!--prettier-ignore-->
2. For polyatomic ions:

    - For each `-` charge, **add** one electron to the **more** electronegative atom.
    - For each `+` charge, **remove** one electron from the **less** electronegative atom

3. Make **terminal atoms** achieve **octet**

<!--prettier-ignore-->
4. Check the **central atom**:
    - Elements from period 2 cannot expand octet. If there are more than 8 electrons, change a double bond to a dative bond
    - Elements from Period 3 onwards can expand octet.
    - Elements in Group 2 and 13 may be electron deficient
    - Element in Group 15 and 17 may have unpaired electrons
