# **Exceptions to the Octet Rule**

## **Electron Deficient Species**

Central atoms can have **fewer** than 8 electrons

## **Expansion of Octet**

- Elements from **period 3 and onwards** can form more than 4 bonds by using their d orbitals
- We say they can **expand its octet** by using **energetically accessible d orbitals**

## **Radical**

- In some molecules, the central atoms have **unpaired electrons**
- Species with **unpaired electrons** are called **radical**
- Radicals are **very reactive**
