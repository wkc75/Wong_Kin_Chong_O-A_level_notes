# **Errors**

## **Accuracy**

<!--prettier-ignore-->
!!! definition "Definition"
    Accuracy is the **closeness** of agreement between a **measured value** and a **true value**.

## **Precision**

<!--prettier-ignore-->
!!! definition "Definition"
    Precision is the degree of **consistency** and **agreement** among **independent measurements of the same quantity**.

<div id="accuracy-precision-target"></div>

## **Random Errors**

<!--prettier-ignore-->
!!! definition "Definition"
    Random errors are **deviations of the measured value from the mean value**, with **varying sings** and **magnitudes**.

**To reduce random errors**

1. Improve the procedure to minimize uncertainty due to random errors. (eg. Measure time taken for 20 oscillation and calculate period instead of measuring one period)
2. Use a large sample size for the mean value to be a good estimate of the true value.

## **Systematic Errors**

<!--prettier-ignore-->
!!! definition "Definition"
    Systematic errors are deviations of the mean value from the true value, with the same sign and similar magnitude

**To reduce systematic errors**

1. Zero/Tare/Calibrate the apparatus
