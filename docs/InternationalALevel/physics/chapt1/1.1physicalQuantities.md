# **Physical Quantities and SI Units**

## **Physical Quantities**

<!--prettier-ignore-->
!!! definition "Definition"
    Physical quantities are quantities that can be measured. They consist of a numerical magnitude and an unit.

## **Base Quantites and their SI Units**

A base quantity is a basic physical quantity that is defined independently while a derived quantity is formed by combining base quantites using a formula.

## **Derived Units**

The units of derived quantities can be expressed in terms of base units using their defining formulae.

$$
Speed = \frac{distance}{time}
$$

- The SI unit of distance is $m$ while the SI unit of time is $s$
- Based on the formula, the unit of the derived quantity speed is $m/s$

# **Homogenity of Physical Equations**

Physical quantities can only be **added**, **subtracted** or **equated** if they have the **same units**.
